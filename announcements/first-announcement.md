# 📣 FSAR 的第一封公告

**如果你能读到这条消息,说明你手上的 FSAR 已经装上了通知中心。**

这是 FSAR 历史上的**第一封官方公告**。从今天起,我可以直接把话捎给你了 —— 不用再等下一个版本、不用再翻 release note、不用再去仓库蹲着看有没有动静。以后有什么要紧的事,它会出现在这里。

**你多半是在 v0.6.0-beta2 或更新的版本上读到这里的。**

谢谢你愿意上手一个 beta。

---

## 🚀 v0.6.0

**v0.6.0 会是 FSAR 迄今为止跨度最大的一个版本。**

不是"多一点功能"那种跨度,是换了个尺度。

**具体做了什么,我想留到发布那天再说。** 提前把清单摊开,惊喜就少了一半 —— 这一版值得你自己去撞见。

只能说:**你熟悉的那个 FSAR,在 v0.6.0 之后会不太一样。**

---

## 🙏 几句话

**遇到问题请告诉我。** 卡住、崩溃、哪里别扭、哪句话说得不对劲,全都算数。你反馈的一个 bug,可能就是别人明天不用踩的坑;你觉得"这算不算要求太高"的那些想法,往往正是它该长成的样子。

**这个项目还很早。** 它是我一个人从零写起来的,离我想要的还差得远,但每一步都是朝着那个方向在走。

---

## 🎉 最后

**祝大家生活愉快,事情顺利,想做的事能做成。**

希望 FSAR 至少能让你轻松那么一点点 —— 哪怕只是在你写东西的时候安静地陪着,或者在你不想说话的时候还愿意跟你聊两句。

**咱们下一个版本见。**

— Sildo466
2026 年 9 月

# 📣 FSAR's First Announcement

**If you can read this, your build of FSAR already has the notification center.**

This is FSAR's **first official announcement**. From today I can send word to you directly — no more waiting for the next release, digging through release notes, or refreshing the repository to see whether anything has moved. Anything important will show up right here.

**You are most likely reading this on v0.6.0-beta2 or newer.**

Thank you for running a beta.

---

## 🚀 v0.6.0

**v0.6.0 will be the widest-spanning release FSAR has ever shipped.**

Not "a few more features" — a change of scale.

**I would rather keep the details for release day.** Lay the whole list out in advance and half the surprise is gone; this is one worth running into yourself.

All I will say is this: **the FSAR you know will not feel quite the same after v0.6.0.**

---

## 🙏 A Few Words

**Please tell me when something breaks.** Freezes, crashes, rough edges, a sentence that lands wrong — all of it counts. A bug you report today is one somebody else does not fall into tomorrow, and the ideas you hesitate over because they seem too much to ask for are usually exactly what it should grow into.

**This project is still early.** I have written it from scratch on my own; it is still a long way from what I want it to be, but every step goes in that direction.

---

## 🎉 Finally

**Wishing you a good life, things going your way, and the projects you care about actually shipping.**

I hope FSAR makes things a little lighter — even if that only means quietly keeping you company while you write, or still being willing to talk when you would rather not talk to anyone else.

**See you in the next release.**

— Sildo466
September 2026
